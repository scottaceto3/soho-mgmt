Why SOHO Management

Built for Providence

We combine local market expertise with enterprise-grade technology to deliver results that consistently exceed expectations — for owners and residents alike.

01

Technology-Powered Operations

One connected platform for every moving part. Owners get real-time financials, maintenance tracking and document access; residents pay rent, sign leases and submit requests online. Less friction, faster answers, full visibility.

02

Local Market Expertise

We know our markets block by block — from South County shores to Southern Mass streets. We price your property precisely, attract qualified tenants fast, and protect your asset year-round.

03

Full-Service Management

Leasing, screening, maintenance coordination, rent collection, annual inspections, and monthly reporting. We handle everything so you earn passive income without the headaches.

04

Rigorous Tenant Screening

Every applicant goes through credit, background, eviction history, and income verification. We place quality tenants who pay on time and respect your investment.

05

Transparent, Simple Fees

No hidden charges or surprise markups. Our straightforward fee structure means you always know what you're paying — and our success is tied directly to yours.

06

Partnered with Smith & Oak RE & SOHO Plumbing

Buying, selling, or repairing? Smith & Oak Real Estate Co. and SOHO Plumbing & Mechanical work alongside us, so brokerage, management and trade services run through one coordinated team. Streamlined service from acquisition to maintenance.
