Living in Warren, RI: Water Street Eats & the Bike Path

November 10, 2026 · SOHO Management

Warren is a small waterfront town on Rhode Island's East Bay with a compact, easy-to-stroll center. Here is a quick look at what's worth doing close to home.

Why Warren Is Likeable

Walk Score rates Warren as "Very Walkable" (79) and "Very Bikeable" (81), so many errands fit into a short stroll or ride. See the Walk Score page for Warren and browse local housing on Zillow's Warren page. Settled in the 1650s, the town still has a historic, shipbuilding-town feel along its rivers.

Coffee & Bites

  • Coffee stop: Cafe Water Street is a well-liked waterfront cafe on the main restaurant strip.
  • Seafood: Blount Clam Shack sits on the Warren waterfront, a spot tied to the Blount food family since the 1940s.
  • Dinner: Revival Craft Kitchen & Bar is a popular Water Street pick.

Night Out

Water Street holds about a dozen restaurants in under half a mile, per a 2022 East Bay RI feature. For a drink, try the fire pits at Bywater or the local Admiral Pub. Check each venue for current hours.

Do This: Ride the East Bay Bike Path

The East Bay Bike Path runs 13.8 miles from Providence's India Point Park to Bristol Harbor, passing through Warren and by Burr's Hill Park. It was the state's first bicycle facility, built in phases between 1987 and 1992.
