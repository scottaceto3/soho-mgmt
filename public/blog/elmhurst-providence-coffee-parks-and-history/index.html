Elmhurst, Providence: Coffee, Parks & Historic Streets

November 18, 2026 · SOHO Management

Elmhurst is a mostly residential neighborhood in northwest Providence, a short trip from downtown. Here is a quick look at what's close to Oakland Avenue.

Why Elmhurst Works

Walk Score gives the Oakland Avenue address a Walk Score of 84 ("Very Walkable"), with a Bike Score of 56 and a Transit Score of 46. Walk Score lists the 57 bus on Smith Street about 0.1 miles away, with the 50 and 56 routes within 0.3 miles and commuter rail about a mile out.

The area grew around a streetcar line that opened in 1882. Pleasant Valley Parkway, created in 1909, was modeled on Blackstone Boulevard, and Providence College has anchored the neighborhood since 1917. Tudor Gothic Nathanael Greene Middle School, built in 1929 on Chalkstone Avenue, is a local landmark.

A Little History: Davis Park

Davis Park began as the estate of Thomas Davis, a Dublin-born jeweler who served in the Rhode Island Senate and the U.S. House. His mansion stood on 34 acres at Chalkstone Avenue and Raymond Street. The city bought the property in 1890 for $75,000 and named it for him. After a VA hospital opened on the land in 1949, eight acres returned to the city in 1953. Today the park has a walking track, community gardens, a baseball field and a statue of César Chávez.

Close by, the Pekin Street Historic District (National Register, 1984) preserves Greek Revival and Victorian two- and three-family homes built between 1870 and 1910. It is a pleasant place to wander on foot.

Coffee & Dinner

  • Coffee: Rise 'N Shine Coffee Bar serves espresso drinks and baked pastries on the first floor of an 1858 carriage house on Holden Street, in neighboring Smith Hill.
  • Dinner: Los Andes on Chalkstone Avenue cooks Peruvian and Bolivian recipes, including chicken and seafood dishes, and offers takeout and delivery.

Night Out

For a casual drink, the Elmhurst Pub at 670 Smith Street is a neighborhood spot with soups and sandwiches on its online menu. Hours vary, so check before you go.
