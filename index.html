Why SOHO Management

Built for Providence

01

AppFolio-Powered Operations

Real-time financial reporting, maintenance tracking, and document access for owners. Online payments, lease signing, and maintenance requests for tenants — all in one platform.

02

Local Market Expertise

We know Providence County — from College Hill to Cranston. We price your property precisely, attract qualified tenants fast, and protect your asset year-round.

Leasing, screening, maintenance, rent collection, inspections, and monthly reporting. We handle everything so you earn passive income without the headaches.
